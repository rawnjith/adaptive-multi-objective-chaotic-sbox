\subsection*{Comment 1: Funding Declaration}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!5!white,colframe=black!50!white,title=\textbf{Editor Comment}]
\textit{Please provide a Funding Declaration in the manuscript. If there was no Funding, please state so.}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=green!5!white,colframe=black!50!white,title=\textbf{Our Response}]
We confirm that this research did not receive any external funding from any funding agency, grant, or individual. No financial support was obtained from governmental, institutional, or private sources for the conduct of this study, the preparation of the manuscript, or the decision to submit it for publication.

To address this comment, we have added a dedicated \textbf{Funding} section to the manuscript, placed between the \textit{Acknowledgements} and \textit{Author information} sections. The section contains the standard Nature Portfolio declaration for unfunded research:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Funding}\\
This research received no external funding.
\end{quote}

\textbf{Location in the revised manuscript:} The Funding declaration appears in the unnumbered \textit{Funding} section after Acknowledgements and before Author information in \texttt{main\_Scientific\_Reports.tex} and the compiled manuscript PDF.

\textbf{Verification:} The declaration reads: ``This research received no external funding.''
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection*{Comment 2: Data Availability Statement in Submission System}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!5!white,colframe=black!50!white,title=\textbf{Editor Comment}]
\textit{We request authors to provide the same Data Availability statement from the manuscript and update in the ``Submission system''.}
\end{tcolorbox}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=green!5!white,colframe=black!50!white,title=\textbf{Our Response}]
The manuscript Data Availability section now reads: ``The simulator, analysis scripts, configuration, paired seed outputs, and figures supporting this study are publicly archived in Zenodo version v1.0.0 (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23439389) and mirrored in the GitHub repository rawnjith/adaptive-multi-objective-chaotic-sbox. No third-party dataset is used.'' The corresponding author should select ``YES'' in the submission-system Data Availability field and paste this statement verbatim so the external field matches the manuscript exactly.
\end{tcolorbox}
